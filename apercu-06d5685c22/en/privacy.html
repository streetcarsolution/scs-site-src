This policy explains what data we collect, for what purposes, how long we keep it and the rights you have. It applies to all services offered by Street Car Solution (Cancún, Quintana Roo, Mexico).

1. Data Controller

  • Yoan Crémona, professional operator of the car rental agency Street Car Solution
    Address: Blvd. Luis Donaldo Colosio KM 13, Local 11, 77565 Cancún, Quintana Roo, Mexico
    Email: reservation@streetcarsolution.com

2. Data We Collect

  • Identification and contact data: last name, first name, email, phone, country.
  • Booking data: dates, pick-up/return locations, vehicle category, selected options.
  • Documents you upload yourself, through a personal and secure link sent by the agency: a copy of the passport and driver's license of each driver (including the date of birth shown on them), a copy of the round-trip flight tickets, and proof of payment (bank transfer or card payment) when a down payment or a security deposit is requested.
  • Messages and exchanges with us: WhatsApp, email, website forms and conversations with Julie, our virtual assistant.
  • Data from website forms (e.g. testimonials): rating, message, consent to publication.
  • Technical browsing data (logs) and preferences (language, currency). Cookies are detailed below.
  • Billing: amounts and payment status. We never ask for a bank card number on our website.

3. Purposes

  • Manage requests, quotes, bookings and the rental contract.
  • Verify your identity and your right to drive from the uploaded documents, and organize the delivery and collection of the vehicle (flight tickets).
  • Provide assistance (before, during and after the rental).
  • Reply to your messages (WhatsApp, email) and your questions, including through Julie, our virtual assistant (artificial intelligence).
  • Send useful information (pre-booking, reminder, vehicle return).
  • Publish certain testimonials, with your consent.
  • Improve the website, prevent fraud and ensure security.
  • Comply with legal obligations (accounting, billing, responses to the competent authorities).

4. Legal Bases

  • Contract performance: processing necessary to manage your request and your rental.
  • Legitimate interest: security, improvement of our services, fraud prevention.
  • Legal obligation: accounting records retention, responses to authorities.
  • Consent: publication of testimonials, use of non-essential cookies.

5. Retention Periods

  • Booking/contract records: duration of the relationship + applicable legal periods.
  • Uploaded documents (passport, license, flight tickets, proof of payment): kept for the legally permitted period, notably to make your future rentals easier.
  • Messages and conversations (WhatsApp, email, Julie): kept to follow up on your file, within the legally permitted period.
  • Accounting/billing: according to the regulations in force.
  • Published testimonials: until you withdraw your consent.
  • Cookies: see the durations in the “Cookies & Trackers” section.

6. Recipients & Transfers

  • Our operations team (bookings, support, billing).
  • Technical providers and usual tools for hosting, email, messaging and forms (e.g. website host, Google services such as Gmail/Sheets/Drive, WhatsApp, CDN/icons), strictly to provide the service.
  • Our booking management tool and its private space, where your documents are stored: they are hosted by secure database and hosting providers (e.g. Supabase, Vercel). Only the agency's team has access to it.
  • Julie, our virtual assistant, works with an artificial intelligence model provider: your messages are sent to it so that it can reply to you. Never write a bank card number or a copy of a document there.
  • Competent authorities if required by law (fines, violations, disputes).
  • As some providers may be located outside your country of residence, we take care to use reputable providers and appropriate mechanisms (standard contractual clauses, internal policies) where required.

7. Cookies & Trackers

  • We use cookies/trackers for:
  • Technical/necessary (e.g. remembering language and currency, site security). Essential for the site to work.
  • Audience measurement (if enabled): to understand how the site is used in order to improve it. Subject to your consent where required.
  • External content (Facebook videos, Google Maps map, Google translation): they are only loaded after your consent, because these services may set their own cookies. You can change your mind at any time using the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of the page.
  • You can manage your preferences from your browser settings (blocking, deletion) and, where applicable, through our banner/preference center. Refusing some cookies may degrade your experience.

8. Your Rights

  • ARCO rights (Mexican personal data protection law, LFPDPPP): Access, Rectification, Cancellation (deletion) and Opposition. They correspond to the rights below.
  • Access: obtain a copy of your data.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: request erasure where the law allows it.
  • Restriction & Objection: under the legal conditions.
  • Withdrawal of consent: at any time for the processing concerned (e.g. a published testimonial).
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured format where applicable.
  • To exercise your rights: reservation@streetcarsolution.com (attach proof of identity if necessary). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security

  • The documents you upload are stored in a private space: you send them through a personal, unique link that closes at the end of the rental, and only the agency's team can view them.
  • We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures (access controls, backups, good hosting practices). However, no system is infallible: we recommend that you stay vigilant when sharing information online.

10. Minors

  • Our services are not intended for unaccompanied minors. Every booking requires a driver who meets the age and license requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this policy to reflect legal or technical developments. The update date will be changed accordingly. In the event of a major change, we may inform you by email or through the website.

For any question about data protection:
Street Car Solution: reservation@streetcarsolution.com
