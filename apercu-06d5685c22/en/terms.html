1. Purpose

  • These terms govern the booking and rental of vehicles offered by Street Car Solution (Cancún, Quintana Roo, Mexico).
  • Any booking implies unreserved acceptance of these terms.

2. Booking & Contract

  • An online booking is a request; it is confirmed by a WhatsApp message and/or a down payment, if requested.
  • The contract is signed at vehicle handover, after verification of the documents (license, passport/ID) of the driver or drivers.
  • A “similar” vehicle may be provided depending on availability.

3. Driver, Age & Documents

  • Minimum age: 21, or 25 for certain models. The driver's license must have been valid for at least 2 years.
  • *Rental is possible for young drivers with an additional fee.
  • Required documents: driver's license, passport.
  • We also ask for a copy of your round-trip flight tickets.
  • An additional driver is possible (see the Services page).

4. Rates, Payment & Security Deposit

  • Rates include insurance and unlimited mileage unless otherwise stated.
  • Payment is made by the accepted methods (cash or debit/credit card).
  • We also accept bank transfers and can send payment links.
  • Rates are set in Mexican pesos (MXN). Foreign currencies are accepted at the exchange rate of the vehicle pick-up day, according to the agency's rate.
  • A security deposit is required at vehicle handover. It is returned after the vehicle is returned, minus any applicable charges (missing fuel, fines, damage, washing, delays, etc.). If these charges exceed the amount of the security deposit, the difference remains the renter's responsibility.

5. Insurance & Liability

  • The level of coverage includes third-party liability (RC / TPL), which covers damage caused to third parties, and occupants' medical expenses. Collision insurance (CDW) and theft insurance (TP) apply to actual costs, within a deductible of 10% of the vehicle's value. Glass breakage is covered with a deductible of 20% of the total replacement invoice (parts and labor).
  • Exclusions: insurance does not apply in cases of drunk driving, driving off the authorized roads or zones (see section 8), negligence or unauthorized use of the vehicle.
  • If you are not responsible for the accident and no exclusion applies, you have nothing to pay.
  • In the event of an accident, theft or incident, the renter must notify Street Car Solution immediately.
  • Assistance in the event of an accident or a problem with the vehicle is free of charge. The travel of our team is billed when the problem is the renter's responsibility (dead battery, lights left on, lost keys, etc.), according to the delivery rates for the location.
  • Under no circumstances is Street Car Solution responsible for the theft of renters' personal belongings left in the vehicles.

6. Fuel

  • The vehicle is handed over with a set fuel level and must be returned at the same level. Otherwise, refueling fees apply. Extra fuel is not reimbursed.

7. Pick-up, Returns & Delays

  • Pick-up and return locations (airport, hotel, etc.) are agreed upon at the time of booking.
  • Vehicle pick-up and/or return between 8:00 pm and 9:00 am incurs an extra fee.
  • Any delay incurs extra charges according to the current rate schedule.
  • A condition inspection (pick-up/return) is carried out; any damage not reported at pick-up may be charged.

8. Vehicle Use

  • The vehicle must be used in accordance with Mexican traffic law.
  • Vehicles must be returned after a car wash; otherwise washing fees will apply.
  • Quintana Roo, Yucatán and Campeche are authorized zones at no extra charge.
  • Chiapas is an authorized zone provided it is approved in advance, and a surcharge will apply.
  • All other zones are not authorized.
  • Taking the vehicle out of the country is prohibited.
  • Any trip outside the authorized zones, or outside the country, is unauthorized use of the vehicle: insurance does not apply, the renter is solely responsible for all costs (damage, theft, towing, repatriation of the vehicle), a surcharge may be billed, and Street Car Solution may end the rental and recover the vehicle at the renter's expense.

9. Fines, Damage & Downtime

  • Fines, tolls and violations during the rental are the renter's responsibility.
  • In the event of damage, repair and/or downtime costs may be charged according to the insurance terms.
  • Minor damage (scratches, scuffs, rocker panels, bumpers, doors, roof, interior, etc.) is deducted from the security deposit. If the cost exceeds the amount of the security deposit, the renter pays the difference: all costs remain the renter's responsibility.
  • Flat tires are the renter's responsibility.
  • Loss of the keys, the vehicle registration card or the license plates is the renter's responsibility: replacement and, if necessary, the travel of our team.

10. Cancellation & No-Show

  • The customer must inform Street Car Solution as soon as possible of any date change or cancellation.
  • Any down payment made before the delivery day is non-refundable.
  • The only exception: a force majeure event beyond the customer's control (natural disasters, government restrictions, serious hospitalization or death), upon presentation of supporting documents. In that case only, a credit will be issued to change the date of the booking, or a refund may be made under certain conditions.

11. Force Majeure

  • Street Car Solution cannot be held liable for non-performance or delay attributable to a force majeure event (unforeseeable external event, natural disaster, destruction of the reserved vehicle, etc.).

12. Partner Agency Vehicles

  • Depending on availability and destination, the reserved vehicle may belong to a Street Car Solution partner agency and be delivered by it.
  • In that case, the rental contract and the condition inspection are drawn up with the partner agency at vehicle handover, and the partner agency keeps the security deposit. Its name appears on the contract.
  • Street Car Solution remains your contact for the booking, assistance and any accident report.
  • Street Car Solution then acts as an intermediary: the partner agency remains responsible for the condition of its vehicle, its delivery and its collection.
  • In the event of a delay, unavailability or vehicle problem attributable to the partner agency, Street Car Solution undertakes to look for a solution without delay (an equivalent vehicle, a new delivery or another arrangement), but cannot be held liable for it, within the limits provided by law.

13. Governing Law & Disputes

  • These terms are governed by Mexican law. Failing an amicable agreement, any dispute will fall under the competent courts of Cancún, Quintana Roo.

14. Personal Data

  • Your data is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

This information is provided for guidance only and does not constitute legal advice. Please check the exact terms on your rental agreement at the time of vehicle handover.
